BabyZoo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Applies to the iOS app and the Android app · Deutsch

BabyZoo is an app for parents and guardians to use together with young children. kudesi, the publisher, collects no personal data through the app: there are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no server for the app. The only connection to another company is the one to the store (Apple or Google Play) for the optional purchase.

1. Who is responsible

kudesi is responsible for this app and this website. The provider details are in the legal notice. Contact for privacy questions: hi@kudesi.de.

2. About the app

BabyZoo is meant to be used by children together with an adult. Purchases, restoring a purchase and links to external websites are behind a short question for grown-ups (a parental gate).

3. Data on your device

The app saves a few settings in its local storage on your device: whether the full version is unlocked, whether sound and music are on, and whether the introduction has been shown. This data stays on the device, is not sent to us or to any server and is deleted when you uninstall the app. Saving it is necessary for the app to work (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). The Android app takes no part in Android's backup.

4. Purchase of the full version

BabyZoo offers an optional one-time in-app purchase that permanently unlocks all animals. The store handles the purchase, the payment and restoring an earlier purchase: the Apple App Store on iPhone, Google Play on Android, under your Apple Account or Google Account and under the store's own terms and privacy policy. We receive no payment details, no name, no email address and no purchase history. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Android: The Google Play Billing library, which is part of the Android app, connects to Google Play to show prices and to process and confirm purchases. It may also send technical diagnostic information about its own operation (for example error and performance data) to Google. We do not receive this information; Google is responsible for it, see Google's privacy policy. When you open the app, it asks Google Play which purchases your Google Account owns, in order to confirm and restore your purchase; the app keeps only the result (unlocked or not) on your device. If an adult in a Google Family Link group has to approve a purchase, Google handles that approval.

5. Permissions

The app asks for no permission that needs your approval: no location, microphone, camera, contacts or photos, and it does not use the advertising ID. On Android the package contains the permissions for Google Play Billing and network access that Google's library and the video player need. iOS requires a microphone usage text because an audio library references the microphone API; the app never uses the microphone and iOS shows no prompt.

6. Children

We collect no personal data from children. The app has no user profiles, no messaging, no location tracking, no camera or microphone recording, no social media integration, no advertising and no analytics.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes your IP address and technical connection data in its server logs under its own privacy statement; we do not receive these logs. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (a secure, available website). The transfer to the USA is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework in which GitHub participates, and on standard contractual clauses (according to GitHub's statement). The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

The links to the privacy policy and the terms in the app open this website in your browser, behind the parental gate.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20), and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). Write to hi@kudesi.de for this. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example in the country where you live or work. The authority responsible for kudesi is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, www.lda.bayern.de. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above. The current version is always at babyzoo.kudesi.de/BabyZoo/privacy.html.
